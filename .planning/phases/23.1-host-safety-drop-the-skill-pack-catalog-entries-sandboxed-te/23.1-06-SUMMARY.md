## Accomplishments

- `assert_gsd_absent(pass)` in `scripts/container-e2e-verify.sh` checks `~/.claude/gsd-core` and `~/.claude/.gsd-source` for a file, dir or dangling link, printing `TRACER_GSD_ABSENT_OK pass=<pass>` or one `TRACER_GSD_PRESENT path=$HOME/<rel>` per hit
