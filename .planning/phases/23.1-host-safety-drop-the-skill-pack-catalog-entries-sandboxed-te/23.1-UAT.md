## Findings

None.

**Observations (by design, or outside 23.1):**

- **O-1 `--guard` with pnpm off PATH.** In one run, `--guard` prints
  `Rewrote the npx redirect …` and then `blocked (pnpm not found): npx`.
  - The launch repair keeps the old body's baked pnpm target, so it never turns a live redirect
    into a hard block. The guard re-apply that follows still resolves pnpm on PATH and writes the
    hard block.
  - This is pre-23.1 guard behaviour. It shows here only because the UAT's fake pnpm is not on
    PATH.
- **O-2 Out-of-shape leftover after removal.**
  - rtk's `rtk.toml` is named under `remove it yourself` in the preview, the detail bar and the
    confirm. After the removal, the result is only `1 removed` (CLI: no line).
  - The rtk receipt is deleted once its proven paths are gone, as D-08 intends. After that, no
    surface names the kept file again.
- **O-3 `space` on a non-selectable retired row gives no status line.** This is the same as for
  other non-selectable Uninstall rows.
- **O-4 Heading form.** The TUI heading renders in the house group form
  `── retired — no longer in the catalog (3)` (with count). The CLI prints the bare
  `retired — no longer in the catalog`.

  `pnpm dlx` download was not run.
- **Hostile input.** A `[red]` or ANSI escape in a receipt id or path.
