<objective>
Casks that update themselves stop looking current while they are outdated on macOS. The refresh pass learns which installed casks auto-update from one batched `brew info` call and, only when one of ours does, asks one batched greedy `brew outdated`; those casks then read outdated, and `u` upgrades exactly them with the greedy flag. Every other cask, and all of Linux, is unchanged.
