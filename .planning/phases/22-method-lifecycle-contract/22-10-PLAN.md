<objective>
The Uninstall view gets the per-row "delete config too" toggle. `c` turns a removable row's config flag on or off; the row shows `[c]`, `config + ` and a detail suffix naming exactly which config paths exist; the removal decision carries exactly the paths every declaring tool agreed to delete. Rows without config, unremovable rows and environment rows say why `c` does nothing.

Purpose: UI-04 in the TUI (the core deletion is 22-09); the review modal and result lines follow in 22-11.

</objective>
