<objective>
Removal follows one per-tool contract. `removal_plan` decides the level from proof alone: a manager uninstall for brew, cask, uv and pnpm only when this installer's receipt names that package (L1); receipt-matched download and app artifacts (L2); a script's vendor `remove` plus its receipt-proven paths (L3); otherwise nothing but a hint. `remove_tool` runs one tool in the locked order (vendor or manager command, then the proven paths, then the receipt), and the CLI and the TUI both remove through it.

Purpose: LIFE-01 removal order and the `remove` argv, LIFE-03 (manager uninstall only by receipt proof), SPEC R2 (one removal seam).
Output: `removal_plan` and the extended `RemovalPlan` in `installer/uninstall.py`; `remove_tool`, `ToolRemoval`, `removal_runs_line`, `UninstallDecision.argv` and both callers in `installer/app.py`; the runner binding in `setup.py`.
</objective>
