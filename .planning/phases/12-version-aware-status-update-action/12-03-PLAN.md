<objective>

Also lands REQ-pnpm-global-reinstall-mitigation's automatic post-pnpm-update trigger (ROADMAP.md Phase 12 note), reusing the already-shipped `installer/pnpm_globals.py::reinstall_node_globals` mechanism from Phases 4 and 5 unchanged.
