<objective>
Make every policy answer its prerequisites and its active state live. The three factories that take an `installed_tools` snapshot also accept a predicate, and `missing_requires_now` re-reads it; `omz_plugins_policy` gets a live `is_active`. 21-17 binds the predicate through `VersionRefreshService.installed_or` (wired, not written, in `setup.py`) and makes `PoliciesScreen.state_refreshed` read these.

Purpose: UI-06's data side (D-12, RESEARCH G3): installing `watch` must unblock the docker tweak on the next Policies entry, and the macOS prune policy must read OFF without its plist.
Output: changes in `installer/policy.py`; tests in three policy test files.
</objective>

Decisions recorded for this plan (Claude's discretion):
- Normalise once at the top of each factory: when `installed_tools` is callable, use it; otherwise wrap the mapping (`None` means an empty mapping) in a lookup that defaults to `False`. Reuse: `Mapping.get`; no new module-level helper unless both factories need the identical three lines, in which case one private `_installed_predicate(installed_tools)` in `installer/policy.py` serves both (name it in the SUMMARY).
- `linux_daemon_policy` has no prerequisites; it keeps discarding the argument and only widens its annotation for signature symmetry.
- `missing_requires` (frozen field) stays populated from the same closure at construction, so nothing that reads the field changes behavior.
- The macOS daemon already binds `is_active=lambda: plist_path.exists()`; this plan pins that contract with a test because the stale reading came from the screen's snapshot, which 21-17 removes.
</context>
