## Accomplishments

- **`installer/diskusage.py`** (new, 124 lines): `DISK_FREE_WARN_PERCENT` (15) and `TMP_USED_WARN_PERCENT` (70) as named module constants; a frozen `DiskPressure` dataclass (`root_free_percent`, `tmp_used_percent`, `daemon: DaemonState`, `tmp_threshold: bool`) with read-only `low_disk`/`high_tmp`/`under_pressure`/`at_risk` properties; `probe_disk_pressure`, an IO-injected pure function (`usage` callable defaulting to `shutil.disk_usage`) that is TOTAL over filesystem and probe-conversion failure (D-03b) and reads the temp mount unconditionally on both settings of `tmp_threshold` (D-03d).
- **`installer/guidance.py`** — `Guidance` gained an `emphasis: bool` field (default `False`, threaded through the hand-written `__init__` via `object.__setattr__`) rather than a fourth `Severity` member. `disk_pressure_guidance(pressure)` implements D-03a (daemon-active-vs-inactive severity split), D-03c (UNAVAILABLE's next_step names only the manual one-off, never offers to enable a nonexistent policy row), always returning at most one item.
- **`installer/render.py`** — `guidance_text` prepends a bold modifier to the resolved `SEVERITY_STYLE` when an item's `emphasis` flag is set; every other branch is byte-identical to before. Since both `render_doctor` (CLI) and `DoctorScreen._tui_guidance` (TUI) already call `guidance_text`, this one change reaches both surfaces.
- **`tests/test_diskusage.py`** (new, 22 tests): every threshold boundary, the macOS/Linux measure-vs-threshold split, both independent unreadable-mount cases, the `at_risk` daemon-state matrix, the zero-total degenerate-mount case, and D-03b's total-vs-propagates contract (parametrized over `OSError`/`PermissionError`/`ValueError`/`TypeError`, plus a malformed non-numeric triple and a propagating `RuntimeError`).
- **`tests/test_guidance.py`** — 17 new tests covering every `DaemonState` × pressure combination, the emphasis/severity pairing, the empty-list cases (no pressure, or both percentages unknown, for every `DaemonState`), and the UNAVAILABLE-vs-INACTIVE next_step distinction (positive: INACTIVE points at enabling; negative: UNAVAILABLE never says enable/Policies/setup).
- **`tests/test_render.py`** — 2 new tests: an emphasised item renders with a `"bold <severity-color>"` style distinct from a plain item's `SEVERITY_STYLE` value; a non-emphasised item renders with exactly its prior style, unchanged.
