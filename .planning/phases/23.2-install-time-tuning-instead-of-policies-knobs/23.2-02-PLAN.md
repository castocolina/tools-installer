Decisions this plan implements:
- D-16: a one-line widget directly under `#execute-log`, shown only while the in-flight step is silent; heartbeats are transient `InstallLogLine`s (they stay in the log, do not count as output, and a stale one is dropped by the generation check). The self-re-arming one-shot check becomes a 1 s tick while silent and keeps the one-pending-timer invariant. `_silence_noted` becomes a counter of heartbeats written this silence (truthy only while a silence is active, reset by output and by the row's end; RESEARCH Pitfall 8). The indicator appears at `SILENCE_SECONDS` and a heartbeat is written at each further 10 s of silence measured from the last output.
- D-17: `ExecuteInputs.wall_clock` (`time.time`, rendered with `time.strftime("%H:%M:%S", time.localtime(...))`) and `ExecuteInputs.ascii_spinner`; elapsed time is whole seconds from the existing monotonic `now`. Rule 4 of `.claude/architecture.md` keeps the decision out of `setup.py`: `wants_ascii_spinner(encoding)` lives in `installer/execute_screen.py`, and `setup.py` only passes `sys.__stderr__`'s encoding.
- Claude's discretion, decided here: the stored heartbeat text is `HH:MM:SS <tool> still running (Ns)` inside the usual `tool │` prefix (RESEARCH Q2 resolution); the indicator reads `<frame> <tool> running · Ns` with Braille frames, and `<frame> <tool> running - Ns` with the ASCII frames, truncated with an ellipsis to the widget width so it never wraps at 80 columns.
- Out of scope: a heartbeat for the headless CLI (SPEC out of scope).
