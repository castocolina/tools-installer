# Phase 24.4 Plan 04: Policies without the guard Summary

Policies no longer offers the pip/npm guard: `ban_policy`, its setup wiring, its detail text and every policy docstring that named it are gone, and the generic `move_link_mode` machinery keeps its full test coverage through a small link-mode-aware test double.

## What changed

- `installer/policy.py`: `ban_policy`, the guards import block, `_RELOAD_HINT` (only the ban used it) and the docstrings naming the ban removed; module, `_rollback` and `tweak_policy` docstrings reworded.
- `setup.py`: `ban_policy` import, `_ban_apply_rc_paths` and the `PolicyInputs.policies` head entry removed. `ban_rc_paths` / `all_ban_rc_paths` stay imported (the `--guard` branch and the legacy sweep still use them until 24.4-06).
- `tests/policy_doubles.py` (new): `BLOCK_BEGIN`, `BLOCK_END`, `block_policy(*, apply_rc_paths, remove_rc_paths, policy_id="block")`. Apply re-reads the (list or callable) live paths and writes a marker block with `apply_block`; remove strips it from the union with `strip_block`; `is_active` is a live probe; `link_mode_aware=True`.
- `tests/test_policy.py`: ban tests deleted; every `move_link_mode` test kept with identical assertions, retargeted to `block_policy` and the `BLOCK_BEGIN`/`BLOCK_END` constants (including the foreign-byte strip and the half-written-block rollback, review H-06).
- `tests/test_policies_e2e.py`: the four guard-behaviour tests deleted; `_build_real_app` and `_snapshot` stay; `_plant_volta_and_pnpm` and every call to it gone; link-mode tests retargeted; new `test_policies_opens_on_the_first_remaining_row`.
- `tests/test_setup.py`: `"ban"` leaves `_KEPT_POLICY_IDS`; the split-mode test keeps its tweak half. `tests/test_policy_tweaks.py` and `tests/test_app.py`: ban halves and the two `run_guard` versus `ban_policy` parity tests deleted.
- `installer/wizard_app.py`: `"ban"` entry of `_policy_detail` deleted; `PoliciesScreen` docstring and the two "unguarded" comments reworded. `installer/ui_common.py`: palette `Policies - env tweaks`. `installer/uninstall.py`: two comments reworded.
- `tests/test_wizard_app.py`: fake policy is id `sample` / `Sample policy` / `sample effect` with a neutral reload hint; the ban detail test deleted; the byte-identical test pins `shell config: sample effect` and `Sample policy — sample effect\nSpace toggles this reversible shell policy.` (confirmed against `_policy_detail` and the Effect-column builder), Docker half unchanged.
