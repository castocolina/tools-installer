# Phase 24.4 Plan 01: Retire the guard at launch Summary

Every launch now removes the sentinel-marked `npm`/`npx`/`pnpm`/`pip`/`pip3` shims and every well-formed `tools-installer ban` block, naming each path, via the new never-raising `installer/legacy_guard.py`; no launch step writes a guard shim or block any more.

- **Task 1 (tracer, 3870b7da):** `installer/legacy_guard.py` (constants moved from `guards.py`, `_is_leftover`, `_strip_ban_blocks`, `remove_ban_blocks`, `remove_guard_shims`); `setup._launch_repairs` composes it; `_warnings_only`, both `rewrite_outdated_*` functions and their tests are gone; `_teardown_cli` runs the repairs as its first statement and prints every repair line; `test_uninstall_screen_passes_the_selected_tools` lost its ban dimension; seven route tests (every route, headless order, foreign/linked npx, user rc lines, notice order, teardown order).
