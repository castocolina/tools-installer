# Phase 24.2 Plan 12: The handover delete Summary

**The Policies daemon path is gone (`daemon_policy`, `linux_daemon_policy`, `ensure_daemon_default`, the job-state worker, `TimePickerScreen`, the old runner asset and the prune-shaped renderers), and every test that guarded it now asserts the same behavior against the schedule engine, the Schedules view or the generic writers; 6882 tests pass at 96.02% coverage.**

## What was built

Task 1 (9fedf09): `PoliciesScreen` loses the `t` and `l` keys, the job-state worker with `JobStatesRead` and its generation counter, `refresh_daemon_state`, `live_state`, the competing warm-up worker with `CompetingProbed`, the log tail, and the daemon entries of the detail copy. `TimePickerScreen` is deleted. The execute-screen comment names `LinkModeScreen`, the modal it mirrors. The Schedules view's probe worker now also warms the competing memo.

Task 2 (fe9a608): `policy.py` loses the two daemon factories, `ensure_daemon_default`, `_record_decided_durably` and the `set_schedule`, `read_schedule`, `state_cache`, `decided`, `clear_decided` fields. `setup.py` loses `_build_daemon_policy` and the constants only it used (`_DAEMON_PLIST_PATH` and `_LINUX_DAEMON_UNIT_DIR` stay for the Doctor's probe and cache). `app.py` and `uninstall.py` lose every `daemon_policy` parameter and the decided-marker clearing. The two policy test files are removed.

Task 3 (dde7239): `prune_daemon_runner.py`, `install_wrapper`, `last_run_summary`, `render_plist`, `write_plist`, `render_service` and `write_units` are deleted. Kept: `remove_wrapper`, `wrapper_present`, `WRAPPER_COMMAND` and its sentinel, `read_schedule`, `read_calendar`, `ensure_log_path` (R1-06), the decided markers, `bootstrap`/`bootout`, the generic renderers and writers, `TimerStateCache`, the competing-unit helpers. The docstrings of `test_prune_tmp_linux_sh.py` and `test_prune_user_tmpdir_sh.py`, and three script comments, now describe the `deleted: N` line as what the schedule runner logs as `ok: deleted N`.
