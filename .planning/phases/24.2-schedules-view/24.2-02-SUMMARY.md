## Notes on the runner

- Log grammar: `<ISO-8601 local with offset, to the second> <id> start|ok: ...|skipped: ...|failed: ...`; cache-cleanup also writes one `ran: <cmd> (exit N|timed out|could not start)` line per manager. Exit 0 for ok and skipped, 1 for failed, 2 for a usage error (no log line written).
- Test-first checks that guard against vacuous tests: `test_tmp_cleanup_keeps_a_file_lsof_reports_open` was verified to delete the orphan (`ok: deleted 1`) when the fake `lsof` prints nothing.
- Cap enforcement: `_append` takes a blocking flock on `<log>.lock`, appends when the line fits, otherwise keeps the newest whole lines (decoded once, measured after re-encoding) in `<log>.<pid>.tmp` and `os.replace`s it; a single line longer than the cap is cut to its newest bytes and still ends in a newline.
- Timeouts: tmp script 600 s, each manager 1200 s, update 1800 s (module constants, lowered by tests through `vars(schedule_runner)`).
