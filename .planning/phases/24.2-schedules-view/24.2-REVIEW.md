## HIGH (fix)

| ID | Source | Finding | Owner |
|----|--------|---------|-------|
| F1 | deep HR-01 | the six calendar schedules default to the same 6 h grid, so they fire together; the run lock never waits, one wins and five log `skipped: busy` at every slot and every catch-up (reproduced with three real runners). Fix: a runner that finds the lock held by **another scheduled run** waits for it (bounded, polling, logged), and skips with `skipped: busy` only when the installer holds the lock or the wait bound expires. SPEC R8 amendment (needs confirmation) | runner |
| F2 | external H-01, deep MR-01, ponytail 2 | macOS: `launchctl bootout` (off, re-time, or a reconcile refresh of a stale plist) kills only the runner; its child runs in its own session and keeps going with the run lock released. Fix: a SIGTERM/SIGINT handler in the runner that kills the active child's process group (the update child stays in-group per R2-04) and logs `failed: interrupted` | runner |
| F3 | external H-02 | a reconcile in another installer process can undo the user's off: `_decision_moved` / `_refresh_in_place` check the decision then release the lock, and `turn_on` writes `decided=True` without re-checking; the Schedules view's toggles do not take the reconcile lock. Fix: user toggles (on/off/re-time/model) take the reconcile lock, and the reconcile's `turn_on` re-checks the decision inside the locked change | engine |
| F4 | external H-03, M-04 | turning off the last schedule can delete `runner.py` and the prune scripts while another process's `turn_on` just adopted them, or while a run is in flight (Linux); the reconcile never repairs it. Fix: shared-asset removal runs under the reconcile lock and only when no schedule unit exists on disk (count by unit name), and is skipped while the run lock is held | engine |
| F5 | ponytail 1, deep MR-03 | copied `runner.py` and prune scripts are never refreshed after a `git pull` (`_is_stale` compares unit text only); a deleted runner fails silently while the row reads `on`. Fix: the reconcile also refreshes a recorded shared asset whose bytes differ from its source (or that is missing), re-recording its fingerprint | engine |
