<objective>
Gap closure for the receipt lookups: one guarded existence check shared by removal and the pass (G23), and a pass that retires a receipt whose every artifact is gone (G10).

Purpose: `plan_removal` and `_receipt_evidence` call `Path.exists()` on the receipt path; on Python 3.11/3.12 that raises `PermissionError` when the receipts dir cannot be searched, aborting the whole refresh or uninstall. Separately, a receipt whose files the user deleted (then reinstalled the tool from dnf) keeps the tool BROKEN forever, because a missing artifact reads as drift.
Output: `receipts.receipt_exists`, `receipts.all_gone`, `receipts.retire`, their callers, and tests.
</objective>
